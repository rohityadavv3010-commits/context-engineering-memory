THE CONTEXT GAP

Code shows what changed.
History explains why.

Important decisions rarely live in one place. Their reasoning gets scattered across the tools teams already use.

01

Context is scattered

Commits, reviews, issues, docs, and incident notes each hold a piece of the decision.

02

People become the index

Every change can interrupt the teammate who still remembers what happened.

03

Explanations drift

Without a visible evidence trail, assumptions slowly become team folklore.
